# Haunted Harbour

![Game Preview](hauntedharbour_gamepreview.gif)

Students will be challenged to develop the game **Haunted Harbour** over the course of 18 weeks or in the form of a 2-week half-day summer camp. 
**Haunted Harbour** is a side-scrolling shoot-em-up starring Viktor Tesla as he blasts his way through a spooky village to defeat the source of the corruption - the lighthouse keeper.

The tools used to create this game are the **Unity Game Engine Software** and within **Microsoft Visual Studio** using the **C#** programming language.

# Learning Goals

The outcome is to develop a student’s love for programming while instilling within them introductory mathematical skills, computer experience and critical thinking which can be taken outside of a class environment.

We at RP4K wish to encourage students who have an interest in coding, math or general curiosity at how things work to explore programming through game development.

Below is a detailed list of what a student will learn throughout our Python UpStream course.

## General Code Skill
- Scope (global variables, access modifiers, static classes)
- Functions (Lambda Expressions)
- Events (Callbacks, Delegates)
- Collections (List, Array, IEnumerable, etc.)
- For Loops (forward and reverse)
- Conditional Statements (ternary operations, if, etc.) 
- Refactoring
- Importing Packages
- Understanding Finite State Machines (movement patterns, attack patterns, etc.)

## Unity Game Engine
- GameObjects and Components
- Understanding Parent and Child Hierarchy
- ScriptableObjects and Monobehaviours
- Understanding Entity Component System
- Rendering in Unity (SpriteRenderer, Animator, AnimationClips etc.)
- Unity Tags and Layers
- Collision based Physics and Raycasting
- Unity Events Functions (Awake, Start, Update, LateUpdate, OnCollisionEnter, etc.)
- Unity Audio System (AudioListener)

## Objected Oriented Programming
- Inheritance (Monobehaviour, Sprite, etc.)
- Understanding struct vs. class

## Math
- Understanding Vectors in Unity (Vector2)
- Trigonometry (angles, radians, cos, sin, rotate around position)
- Cartesian Grid and 2D Graphs
- Math Operations (Mathf class usage, arithmetic)
- Understanding Time.deltaTime, FixedUpdate, FPS

# Instructional Method
Students participate in Live Virtual Sessions through a video program called Zoom.
These sessions are the primary form of learning, where an instructor guides the student
through the course material. Our class sizes are limited to a maximum of 4 students per
instructor and take place between 1-2 hours once per week at a scheduled time and
Day.

Students also have access to our online resources through RP4K’s Canvas Portal.
Extra material in the form of quizzes, discussions, assignments, and other materials are
provided for additional learning outside of the Live Virtual Sessions. Previous course
recordings are uploaded and students can participate in class discussions or contact the
instructor with any questions they may have.
